# Founder seeding

Okavo does not ship sample requirements, sample developers, testimonials, or fake activity.

`node scripts/founder-status.mjs` reads live D1 counts and prints the hand steps. It does not write rows. If D1 cannot be read, the counts are omitted and the script exits non-zero.

Do these yourself:

1. Sign up at https://okavo.org/signin with the owner email and confirm the code. The Worker promotes `fakhruddinkhambaty@gmail.com` and `fktiindia@gmail.com` to admin when that account verifies. Do not insert a password.
2. Post a requirement only if you will pay the posting fee and fund the work. There is no seed command for this.
3. Recruit a developer by asking them to create their own account, upload their own identity document, and take the build exam. Do not approve an identity you have not reviewed.
4. Read the admin liquidity panel. Zero means that step has not happened.

Identity review is a person. A build exam at 70 or above may auto-approve 48 hours after submission unless auto-approval is paused or that exam is on hold. Anything else waits. Identity files are deleted 90 days after the decision.
